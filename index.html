Inspired by Felix Rieseberg's room.

The room is modelled with Blender's Python API and rendered with Cycles. The view is the Shanghai Bund HDRI; the plant, books, photo frame and the wall and wood textures are from Poly Haven (CC0).

The display, keyboard, lamp and the rest are modelled in code. Rain and sound are generated in the browser.
